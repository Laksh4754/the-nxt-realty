THE NXT REALTY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains, in general terms, how The NXT Realty website handles information submitted through its property enquiry forms and contact links. This is a launch draft and must be reviewed by the business and, where appropriate, a qualified legal professional before publication.

Information you may provide

When you submit an enquiry, the website may collect your name, phone number, property requirement, preferred location, message and consent to be contacted. The website may also record the source and time of the enquiry.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond to your request, share relevant property information, arrange callbacks or site visits, and manage customer enquiries. The website may open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message so you can continue the conversation.

Storage and service providers

Website enquiries and property information are processed using the website's configured Supabase database and hosting services. When you choose to use WhatsApp or open an external brochure, map or video link, that service's own privacy terms apply. The business should identify and review its service providers and retention arrangements before launch.

Property information

Property details, photographs, prices, carpet areas, amenities, possession information and registration details are provided for general information and may change. Please independently verify all material details with the developer or relevant authority before making a decision.

Retention and your requests

The business should retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably required for the stated purposes or applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, contact The NXT Realty using the WhatsApp contact shown on the website. The business should confirm and publish its formal contact details and process before launch.

Cookies and analytics

This starter website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers, embedded content or future analytics tools may process technical information; this policy must be updated if such tools are added.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact The NXT Realty through the WhatsApp contact link on this website. Before public launch, add the business's official email address and business address here.

Business owner action before launch: confirm the actual data collected, who can access enquiry records, retention period, official contact details, hosting and messaging providers, and any analytics or advertising tools. Do not publish this draft without that review.
