Privacy information.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page explains how information entered into this website preview is handled.

Website enquiry form

This preview does not send or store your form details on a website server. When you submit the enquiry form, WhatsApp opens with a message containing the details you entered. Please review the message and choose whether to send it. Information you send through WhatsApp is then handled according to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

What information may be included?

If you choose to send the message, it may include your name, phone number, optional email, preferred location, property requirement, property type and budget selection.

Choice and contact

You can close WhatsApp without sending the message. Do not enter information you do not want to share. For questions about a property enquiry, contact The NXT Realty through the official business WhatsApp link on the homepage.

Important note

This is a starter privacy notice for the current static website. Before running paid advertising or collecting enquiries through any additional service, the business should review and update this notice to reflect its actual data handling practices and contact details.
